# Privacy Policy — Attendance Last updated: 6th September 2026 Attendance is an internal workforce attendance and location-tracking tool used by us to record employee work sessions. ## Information We Collect - **Name and phone number**, provided by the employee at login. - **Precise location data**, recorded periodically while an employee has an active, self-started work session — including while the app is in the background or closed. Location is never recorded outside an active session. - **Photographs (selfies)**, taken directly by the employee using their device camera, only at the start and end of a work session, to confirm the identity of the person clocking in and out. ## How We Use This Information This data is used solely to verify employee attendance, work location, and route during active work sessions, and to allow designated administrators within [Your Company Name] to review this information. It is not used for any other purpose. ## Data Sharing We do not sell this data. We do not share it with any third party, advertiser, or external organization. Data is stored securely with our backend provider (Supabase) and is only accessible to authenticated administrators within [Your Company Name]. ## Data Retention and Deletion Location, session, and photo data is retained until an administrator removes it, or the employee's record is deleted from the system, at which point all associated location history, session records, and photographs are permanently deleted. ## Employee Consent Location tracking and selfie capture are initiated directly by the employee via an explicit action (starting a work session) and can be stopped by the employee at any time.
